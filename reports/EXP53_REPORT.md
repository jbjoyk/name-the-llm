## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

EXP53 executes a rigorous, scientifically controlled exploration of the fusion weight surface between **Qwen3-8B (q8v5)** and **Qwen3-14B (q14v5)**, branching directly from the validated **EXP52 dual-LoRA architecture**.

### Context & Motivation
- **EXP52 Kaggle Public LB: 0.632** (achieved using fixed weights $w_8 = 0.60, w_{14} = 0.40$).
- In EXP51 and EXP52, the local 5-fold prompt-disjoint out-of-fold (OOF) Balanced Accuracy reached **0.7873 at $w_{14} = 0.40$** (+0.1963 over standalone EXP47's 0.5910).
- However, as established in the competition's epistemic guidelines, the local OOF score is substantially higher than Kaggle Public LB due to the difference between dense prompt holdouts and sparse hidden test conditions ($R \approx 2$).
- **EXP53 Objective**: Systematically evaluate the full parametric spectrum $w_{14} \in \{0.00, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60\}$ on the exact verified prompt-disjoint 5-fold OOF predictions to characterize:
  1. The shape and flatness of the OOF performance curve.
  2. The statistical uncertainty via paired bootstrap hypothesis testing (2,000 resamples).
  3. The fold-level variance and stability.
  4. Whether $w_{14} = 0.40$ remains the optimal Kaggle candidate, or whether a nearby weight provides a superior risk-adjusted profile.

### Experimental Controls Upheld
- **Strictly Immutable Architecture**: Zero changes were made to base models, adapters, truncation depths (keep=24 for 8B, keep=27 for 14B), pooling layers, classification heads, tokenizers, EOS token (`151643`), or prompt recovery clustering.
- **Zero Extraneous Components**: Strictly NO LinearSVC, NO TF-IDF, NO Sinkhorn, NO ATLAS, NO typography, and NO pairwise verifiers.
- **Pure Re-use of Validated Predictions**: Inference was performed directly on the frozen, verified OOF prediction arrays without refitting or test data contamination.
- **No Kaggle Submission**: Per explicit user directive, no external submissions were created.

## 3. Mathematical Definition & Fusion Formulation

Following the exact EXP52 formulation, the ensemble operates purely in **log-probability space**:

$$\log P_{\text{fused}}(c \mid x) = (1 - w_{14}) \cdot \log P_{q8}(c \mid x) + w_{14} \cdot \log P_{q14}(c \mid x)$$

where:
- $w_{14} \in \{0.00, 0.10, 0.20, 0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60\}$
- $w_8 = 1.0 - w_{14}$
- Probabilities are extracted via numerically stable softmax:
  $$P_{\text{fused}}(c \mid x) = \frac{\exp\left(\log P_{\text{fused}}(c \mid x) - \max_k \log P_{\text{fused}}(k \mid x)\right)}{\sum_{j=1}^{18} \exp\left(\log P_{\text{fused}}(j \mid x) - \max_k \log P_{\text{fused}}(k \mid x)\right)}$$
- Individual model probabilities $P(c)$ are bounded below by $\epsilon = 10^{-15}$ before the logarithm to prevent numerical underflow.
- No temperature scaling, power transforms, or uncalibrated logit offsets were introduced.

### Statistical Insights:
- **$w_{14} = 0.40$ vs $w_{14} = 0.50$**: The point difference is only **2 samples** out of 2,699 (2,125 correct vs 2,123 correct, $\Delta = -0.0008$). The 95% bootstrap confidence interval $[-0.0091, +0.0074]$ is centered virtually on zero, and $P(\Delta > 0) = 0.4215$. The performance of 0.40 and 0.50 is statistically identical.
- **Degradation Beyond 0.50**: At $w_{14} = 0.60$, performance drops by $-0.0130$ BA, and the 95% confidence interval $[-0.0244, -0.0018]$ excludes zero ($P(\Delta > 0) = 0.0135$). Weights above 0.50 definitively hurt performance.
- **Degradation Below 0.40**: At $w_{14} = 0.30$, performance drops by $-0.0185$ BA ($P(\Delta > 0) = 0.0000$). Under-weighting the 14B model discards significant orthogonal predictive power.

## 10. Epistemic Principle & Strategic Decision

**Core Observation**:
The OOF performance maximum occurs at **$w_{14} = 0.40$**, which is the **exact weight already instantiated and submitted in EXP52 (Kaggle LB 0.632)**.

The adjacent weight $w_{14} = 0.50$ is statistically equivalent ($\Delta = -0.0008$, $P = 0.4215$), while weights below 0.40 or above 0.50 are strictly inferior.

Therefore:
1. The weight $w_{14} = 0.40$ used in EXP52 is **fully optimal**.
2. There is no evidence justifying modifying the fusion weight away from 0.40.
3. Spending a precious Kaggle submission on $w_{14} = 0.50$ would represent submission budget waste for a statistically indistinguishable delta ($\Delta = -0.08$ percentage points on OOF).
4. **EXP52 remains the undefeated, optimal production baseline.**
